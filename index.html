About Santa Cruz

📍 Location

Santa Cruz is a coastal municipality in Occidental Mindoro, Philippines. Coordinates: 12.5°N, 121.1°E

🌡️ Climate

Tropical Maritime Climate with average temperatures of 25-32°C year-round. High humidity and warm weather throughout the year.

🌧️ Rainy Season

June to October: Southwest Monsoon (Habagat) brings significant rainfall. Afternoon showers and occasional thunderstorms are common.

☀️ Dry Season

November to May: Northeast Monsoon (Amihan) brings drier conditions. Perfect for outdoor activities with lower humidity.

🌪️ Typhoon Season

June to November: Peak typhoon season in the region. Residents should stay alert and prepared during this period.

🌊 Water Temperature

The Sulu Sea maintains warm waters year-round, averaging 27-29°C. Ideal for swimming and water activities.
